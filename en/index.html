Your property in Brazil, handled from wherever you are

Brazilian property records work differently from most countries: ownership is proven by the registration (matrícula) at the Real Estate Registry Office, not by the purchase contract. Before buying, selling or inheriting, the documents must be checked and often updated. We handle the technical and documentary side remotely and keep you informed in English.

How we help

  • Due diligence before buying: registry certificates, liens, taxes and seller checks
  • Obtaining and updating property certificates
  • Following deeds and registration until the property is in your name
  • Regularizing constructions, areas and rural registrations (CAR, CCIR, georeferencing)
  • Coordinating with independent lawyers when legal acts are required (e.g., probate, adverse possession)

Foreigners need a Brazilian taxpayer number (CPF) for most property transactions. Legal advice is provided only by independent licensed attorneys.
